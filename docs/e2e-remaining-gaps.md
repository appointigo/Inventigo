# Stockiva remaining non-WhatsApp discovery gaps

Pre-implementation classification recorded on 2026-10-07. This table accounts for the **9 unit-only, 14 deferred, and 6 blocked** scenarios carried by the prior 130-scenario matrix. It was written before adding coverage in the final discovery pass. “Safe” means the behavior can be tested without production changes, external providers, or any database other than `stockiva_e2e`.

## Classification decisions

- Safe now: **22** scenarios (including bounded CRUD portions of EXP-05 and INT-07).
- Not safely automatable without a new seam or product decision: **7** scenarios or scenario portions: AUTH-08, AUTH-09, EXP-05 recurrence execution, INT-07 sale usage, SEC-10, INT-10, and EXT-01.
- External-provider tests remain prohibited. No WhatsApp route or feature is included here.
- The authorized reorder cron is now safe because the final brief explicitly permits an E2E-only secret and the route performs database-backed alert evaluation rather than external delivery.
- Platform administration is now safe because the final brief explicitly permits direct deterministic SUPER_ADMIN seeding in the disposable database.
